# Fincake

**Portfolio tracking and stock research, together in one Android app.**

Fincake was built to make it easier to understand your portfolio without jumping between shallow trackers and cluttered research tools. Add positions manually or import them from a CSV, then explore your portfolio and the companies you follow.

## What you can do

- **Track portfolio performance** — review annual returns or a selected period and compare results with an index.
- **Explore portfolio fundamentals** — compare key metrics, such as portfolio P/E, with the market.
- **Build forecast scenarios** — explore pessimistic, neutral, and optimistic scenarios based on your historical performance.
- **Create company projections** — model a company using your expected revenue and net income growth.
- **Compare stocks** — view up to three companies side by side.
